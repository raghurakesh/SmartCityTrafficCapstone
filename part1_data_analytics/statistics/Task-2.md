# Interpretation of Traffic Volume Statistics

1. The large difference between Standard deviation Mean indicates that traffic volume is highly volatile across the day. Its possibly due to high traffic during day time peak hour traffic, and quiet night time traffic, in some cases it is 0, as the Minimum indicates.\

2. Median is higher than the mean. This happens because non peak time or night time traffic (0 at times) is dragging the overall average or mean down.

# Task 2.2 - Intepretation of the Correlation

1. Direction - The number is positive (+0.1303) indicating busier roads are seen in warmer temperatures.
2. Strength - Its a very weak coefficient. The number is very close to 0, which means that the impact of temperature to traffic is very low, and does not provide a clear reason for traffic changes.
3. Why correlation does not necessarily imply causation - People normally use their vehicles during the day time, and this is time when the temperature is higher compared to lower temperatures in the night time or non-peak time. The factors driving the traffic are other factors, and not dependent on the temperature itself.
