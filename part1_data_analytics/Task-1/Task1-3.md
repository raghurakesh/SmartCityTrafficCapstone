# Notable year-on-year change in temperature

1. Labor Day - Thats late summer (early September). Highly consistent warmth across all 3 years between 20.02 Celsius and 22.39 Celsius with very little variation.

2. New Year's Day - Thats mid winter. Extreme freezing conditions in both years where recordings are available (2016, 2017). Temperature was -7.21 Celcius in 2016 and -2.53 in 2017.

# Relevance to traffic conditions

1. On Labor day, despite pleasant weather, the traffic readings where consistenly lower compared to normal days. This shows that scheduled holidays results in lesser people commuting due to school/business closures.

2. On New Year day, due to the freezing conditions couple with the holiday drove traffic down even further .
